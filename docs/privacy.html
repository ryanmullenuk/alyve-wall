Privacy policy

Updated 20 October 2026. Applies to ALYVE WALL version 1.0 for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and these web pages.

Who is responsible

ALYVE WALL is developed by Ryan Mullen under the Redhead Games name. For privacy questions or requests, email ryanmullenuk@yahoo.co.uk.

What the app collects

Nothing. ALYVE WALL has no account, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send your designs, images or exports to any server. Wallpapers are created on your device.

Your designs and images

Your current design and any image you add are stored on your device so they are there next time you open the app. They are removed if you delete the app. Images you pick are read only through the system photo picker, which shares just the photo you choose.

Photos

If you choose Save to Photos, the app asks for permission to add to your photo library and uses it only to save the wallpapers, videos and Live Photos you create.

Purchases

Apple processes the Premium purchase. The app checks Apple's verified purchase information to unlock Premium. Redhead Games does not receive your payment details or Apple Account password. See Apple's privacy information.

If you contact support

If you email support, Ryan Mullen receives your email address, message and any attachments, and uses them only to respond to you. Correspondence is handled through Yahoo Mail and kept only as long as needed. You can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of this information, and you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

These web pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver them. They contain no analytics, advertising cookies or tracking scripts.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. The date above shows the latest version.
