Set a Live Photo as your Lock Screen

In ALYVE WALL, tap Export wallpaper, choose Live Photo and create it. It's saved to Photos. Then open Settings › Wallpaper › Add New Wallpaper › Photos, choose it and make sure the Live Photo button is on. Touch and hold the Lock Screen to see it move.

Use a video on your Mac

Export an MP4 in a Mac size, save it to a folder or AirDrop it to your Mac, and set it with the video-wallpaper app of your choice. Every video loops seamlessly.

Restore Premium

Premium is a one-off purchase shared across your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Open the crown screen and tap Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Photos access

ALYVE WALL asks for permission to add to your photo library only so it can save the wallpapers you create. You can change this in Settings › Apps › ALYVE WALL › Photos.
